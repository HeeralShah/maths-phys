# Publish complete chapter editions with anchored annotation layers

Prepare and publish one complete chapter (or an entire chapter-sized paper/document) as an immutable edition, while keeping faithful source blocks separate from generated, historical, and learner annotation layers. Comments attach to exact source spans or transitions; derivation bridges sit between the formula lines they explain; small checkable items carry learner-owned dated checks. This replaces the page-oriented opaque Markdown/card model because that model cannot preserve source/comment identity, formula correspondence, or the user's interlinear study method.
