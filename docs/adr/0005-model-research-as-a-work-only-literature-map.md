# Model research as a work-only literature map

The research canvas contains bibliographic works related to one focal paper, not headings, assumptions, results, reading passes, or other internal parts of the same document. Internal paper analysis remains first-class evidence and provides subsection/result filters and relationship context off-canvas. Bare citation facts remain distinct from claims such as uses, extends, generalises, corrects, or influences; the latter require explicit evidence and epistemic status. This replaces the current same-document graph and prevents arbitrary internal structure from becoming visually meaningless graph topology.
