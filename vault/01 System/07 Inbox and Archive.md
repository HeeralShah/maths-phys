# Inbox and Archive

The vault keeps rough capture and retired material separate from the Atlas.

## Inbox

`90 Inbox` is for material that has not yet been properly placed in the knowledge tree.

Typical uses:

- quick topic capture
- temporary course intake
- rough synthesis drafts
- notes that still need a proper branch

The goal is to move useful material from Inbox into the Atlas once its topic is clear.

## Course Intake

Course material is not a separate permanent system.

If course notes are useful, they should eventually be rewritten or redistributed into topic branches inside the Atlas.

Current intake area:

- [[90 Inbox/02 Course Intake/01 Course Intake Index|Course Intake]]

## Archive

`99 Archive` stores retired structures, failed experiments, and old organizational ideas that should not clutter the active vault.

Archive material may remain valuable for reference, but it should not drive active navigation.

## Rule Of Thumb

- active knowledge belongs in Atlas
- unfinished intake belongs in Inbox
- retired structures belong in Archive
