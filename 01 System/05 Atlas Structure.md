# Atlas Structure

`02 Atlas` is the actual knowledge base.

## Main Principles

- The Atlas is one continuous knowledge tree.
- Mathematics and physics are not separated into parallel top-level systems.
- Index pages live inside the branches they organize.
- Branches should be created for conceptual usefulness, not because a discipline label demands them.

## Root Structure

- [[02 Atlas/01 Atlas Index|Atlas Index]] is the root of the active knowledge tree.
- First-level branches may mix mathematics and physics naturally.
- A branch may hold topic pages, proof pages, example pages, synthesis pages, sub-branches, and source material relevant to that branch.

## Sub-Branches

Every meaningful branch should eventually have:

- an index page at the top
- clear subtopic links when the branch becomes large enough
- locally relevant source material in the branch itself

## Working with Sources

- Source files should live inside the branch they inform, usually in `99 Sources`.
- Provisional reading notes, quotations, and source-driven examples can live in `98 Source Study` before they are reorganized into permanent topic, proof, or example pages.
- This keeps source work close to the branch while still giving us a staging area for rough understanding.

## What Not To Build

- no separate top-level mathematics system
- no separate top-level physics system
- no separate global source library
- no separate top-level index layer outside the Atlas
