# Personal Study System

This glossary defines the concepts used to discuss the study vault and its proposed application.

## Study materials

**Source**:
An original document or other reference material from which study content is derived. A source retains its own wording, order, and notation.

**Reading edition**:
A navigable, source-faithful rendering whose annotation layer is attached to exact passages, equations, figures, and gaps between source blocks. It preserves the source's content, order, and notation while allowing reflow for readability.

**Preparation scope**:
The complete contiguous source range selected for one preparation operation: normally a chapter, or the whole document when it is already a chapter or paper.
_Avoid_: Selected pages, generation unit

**Chapter edition**:
An immutable, completely published reading edition for one preparation scope. Partial page output is never exposed as a finished chapter edition.
_Avoid_: Page edition, note card

**Source block**:
An ordered faithful unit of source content, such as a heading, paragraph, equation, figure, table, example, or exercise, used for rendering and anchoring.
_Avoid_: Study unit

**Annotation anchor**:
A durable pointer from an annotation to a source block, quoted range, formula, figure region, or the gap between two source blocks.
_Avoid_: Text match, character offset

**Derivation bridge**:
A structured annotation placed between source formula lines that exposes omitted transformations, corresponding terms, and the result or rule used.

**Checkable item**:
A small coherent claim, equation group, proof move, definition, or paragraph cluster against which the learner can record a dated understanding check.
_Avoid_: Chapter check, generated blue mark

**Atlas**:
The set of concise, canonical topic notes that connect knowledge across sources and use one consistent notation.

**Canonical note**:
A human-readable Atlas note that states the current synthesis of a topic, with links to supporting sources and study context.

**Source provenance**:
The traceable relationship between a claim or passage and the source material that supports it.

## Study and synthesis

**Study check**:
A dated record of the learner's assessed understanding of one checkable item. Only the learner can create an understood state; an agent-authored annotation cannot imply it.

**Signoff**:
The user's explicit confirmation that a study unit is ready to contribute to a canonical synthesis.

**Refresher**:
A compact review of currently studied material, limited to at most one page.

**Hint ladder**:
The ordered assistance levels for a problem: orientation, small hint, strong hint, partial solution, and full solution.

## Research literature

**Work**:
A bibliographically identifiable paper, book, thesis, or set of notes. A work may have a local source file but does not require one.

**Literature map**:
A revisioned, evidence-backed view of external works related to one focal work. Its visible nodes are works, never the focal paper's internal sections, results, assumptions, or reading steps.
_Avoid_: Research graph, concept graph

**Paper analysis record**:
A result, assumption, contribution, method, limitation, or section summary belonging to one paper. It can filter or explain a literature relationship without becoming a map node.

**Literature relationship**:
An evidence-backed claim about how two works relate, kept distinct from the bare bibliographic fact that one cites the other.
