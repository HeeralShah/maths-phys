# Personal Study System

This glossary defines the concepts used to discuss the study vault and its proposed application.

## Study materials

**Source**:
An original document or other reference material from which study content is derived. A source retains its own wording, order, and notation.

**Reading edition**:
A navigable study rendering of source material that preserves the original content and order while allowing paragraph layout and study annotations to improve readability.

**Atlas**:
The set of concise, canonical topic notes that connect knowledge across sources and use one consistent notation.

**Canonical note**:
A human-readable Atlas note that states the current synthesis of a topic, with links to supporting sources and study context.

**Source provenance**:
The traceable relationship between a claim or passage and the source material that supports it.

## Study and synthesis

**Study unit**:
A coherent piece of material selected for study, such as a subsection or chapter. Agents may recommend its boundaries from examples; the user can inspect the resulting unit.

**Study check**:
A dated record of the learner's assessed understanding of material. Initial checks are dated; later checks support quiet recall and refresher review.

**Signoff**:
The user's explicit confirmation that a study unit is ready to contribute to a canonical synthesis.

**Refresher**:
A compact review of currently studied material, limited to at most one page.

**Hint ladder**:
The ordered assistance levels for a problem: orientation, small hint, strong hint, partial solution, and full solution.
